About biblical counseling

What makes biblical counseling different from other types of counseling?
  • Divine wisdom for life's complexities. Scripture provides God's own perspective on your struggles, offering unchanging truth and reliable guidance that transcends human insight and cultural trends.
  • Heart transformation, not just relief or management. We believe God's Word has power to create real change at the deepest level, transforming the desires, beliefs, and loves that shape your life.
  • Addressing root issues. Rather than managing symptoms, we explore the heart-level dynamics (your deepest desires, fears, and worship) that drive patterns in your life.
  • Living from gospel truth. Real change flows from understanding and embracing what Christ has accomplished for you, not from trying to improve yourself through willpower and effort.
  • Connected to your church community. Biblical counseling strengthens your relationships within the body of Christ, fostering growth that continues beyond counseling sessions.
What makes biblical counseling different from therapy?

We apply Scripture to life's challenges rather than psychological techniques. Our counselors are trained in biblical counseling methodology, not licensed as mental health professionals.

Are your counselors actually qualified?

Our counselors have extensive training in biblical counseling and theology. They're not state-licensed therapists, they're trained ministers of God's Word who specialize in applying Scripture to life's challenges.

The counseling process

What does the counseling process look like?

Counseling unfolds in phases. Early sessions (2–4) focus on understanding your story and establishing biblical hope. Middle sessions involve interpreting your problem biblically and developing strategies for lasting change. Final sessions review what you've learned and send you forward with encouragement.

Each session includes prayer, homework review, focused work on your issue, and planning next steps. Between sessions, expect homework: Scripture memory, reading, prayer practices, or developing rhythms that support lasting change. We believe transformation happens through the ordinary means of grace (God's Word, prayer, and community) so our counseling deepens your engagement with these, rather than replacing them.

How long does this take?

Depends on the situation. Some find significant help in a handful of sessions. Others benefit from longer-term guidance. We'll work together to determine what serves you best.

Do I need to be a church member?

Not at all. We're here to serve anyone willing to engage with Scripture's wisdom for their struggles.

Is this really confidential?

Yes, with biblical and legal limits. Our consent form explains specific situations where confidentiality might be broken, mainly safety concerns and legal requirements.

Cost and practical details

What does it cost?

The initial 20-minute consultation is free. Sessions are $100. Members of partner churches receive reduced rates, and if the fee presents a genuine hardship, please talk with us.

Do you take insurance?

No. Because biblical counseling is religious ministry rather than licensed therapy, insurance doesn't cover sessions.

Can we meet virtually?

Yes. Virtual sessions are conducted via the secure client portal and follow the same format, duration, and fees as in-person sessions.

The first conversation is free.

All new clients begin with a free 20-minute consultation. Tell us what's going on, and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.
