How we help families

Parents shape the home

We counsel parents, children, and teens. Because parents set the tone of a household, we'll usually involve you, even when it's your child we're counseling.

Hearts, not just behavior

Rewards and consequences have their place, but real change happens at the level of the heart, for children and parents alike.

Fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord.

Ephesians 6:4

A plan for your home

Expect clear, workable next steps: how to talk, how to correct, how to repair, and how to keep going when it's hard.

Questions

Do you counsel children and teens?

Yes, when parents want us to. Because parents shape the home, we'll usually involve you along the way. Tell us about your family in the free consultation and we'll talk through what makes sense.

My spouse and I don't agree on parenting. Can we still come?

Yes. It's one of the most common reasons parents come, and getting on the same page is often the most important first step.

Can you help with an adult child?

Yes. Parenting changes when children grow up, and so do the heartaches. We help parents navigate strained relationships, prodigals, and boundaries with adult children.
