What is biblical counseling?

In short

Biblical counseling is personal ministry of God's Word. A trained counselor helps you understand what you're facing and respond to it through Scripture, with Christ at the center.

What it is

Personal ministry of God's Word: honest conversation, prayer, and practical homework, shaped by Scripture rather than psychological techniques.

What it isn't

Licensed therapy. We don't provide psychotherapy or mental health diagnosis, and sessions aren't billed to insurance.

Who it's for

Christians from any church, and anyone willing to engage with Scripture's wisdom for their struggles.

Where it leads

Transformation, not simply relief: a changed heart and a life ordered under Christ's lordship.

Biblical counseling and therapy

We apply Scripture to life's challenges rather than psychological techniques. Our counselors are trained in biblical counseling methodology, not licensed as mental health professionals.

Our goal is transformation, not simply relief. Rather than managing symptoms, we go to the heart: what you love, fear, and worship, and to Christ, who changes people from the inside out.

Our philosophy

Foundation

Scripture informs our understanding.

  • God's Word speaks with authority and sufficiency to all of life's challenges.
  • We use biblical categories (the heart, sin, suffering, and grace) to understand your struggles.
  • Scripture interprets everything, including our own experiences.

Framework

Scripture guides our methodology.

  • We address root issues at the level of the heart, not just surface behaviors.
  • We counsel struggles rooted in sin and struggles rooted in suffering differently.
  • Change happens through the ordinary means of grace: God's Word, prayer, and community.

Scripture is both our source and our system; the foundation that informs our understanding and the framework that guides our methodology. We don't just use Bible verses, we use biblical categories to understand your struggles and biblical solutions to address them.

Our goal extends beyond symptom relief to worldview formation and heart transformation, helping you see and respond to life through God's eyes rather than the world's categories.

All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness, that the man of God may be complete, equipped for every good work.

2 Timothy 3:16–17

Our commitments

Five commitments that hold up everything we do.

  • Scripture-driven

    God's Word defines everything about our counseling. We believe the Bible speaks with authority and sufficiency to all of life's challenges, providing both the lens to understand your struggles and the resources to address them.

  • Heart-focused

    We deal with the real issues, not just surface behaviors. Our goal is transformation of the inner person: your thoughts, desires, beliefs, and motives. Expect deep work that doesn't settle for quick fixes or shallow optimism.

  • Christ-centered

    All lasting change flows from the gospel of Jesus Christ. He has made us more than conquerors. Not through techniques or formulas, but through knowing Christ and being transformed by Him. The gospel reshapes everything.

  • Wisdom-guided

    No two situations are identical. We apply Scripture's permanent principles with discernment to your unique circumstances, pursuing what's truly loving and helpful for you. No cookie-cutter solutions or formulas.

  • Church-connected

    Biblical counseling works best when integrated into the local church, not separated from it. We're an aid to your church, not a replacement. With your permission, we coordinate with pastors to ensure consistent biblical care.

Our distinctives

What sets YCBC apart.

Theologically grounded care

Seminary-level training in theology and biblical interpretation, so Scripture is handled rightly.

Read moreShow less

Our counselor brings seminary-level training in theology and biblical interpretation. This isn't just good intentions, it's rigorous theological grounding that equips us to handle Scripture rightly and apply it to life's complexity.

Tailored to specific issues

We counsel struggles rooted in sin and struggles rooted in suffering differently.

Read moreShow less

We distinguish between struggles rooted primarily in sin and those rooted primarily in suffering, counseling each differently. Sin requires confession, confrontation, conformity to Christ, and commitment to change. Suffering calls for moving toward Christ, mourning with Him, maturing through trials, and eventually ministering comfort to others.

Church-integrated ministry

We work alongside your pastors and elders, never in place of them.

Read moreShow less

YCBC is part of the local church. With your permission, we coordinate with your pastors to ensure consistent care. When conflicts arise between our guidance and your church leadership, we defer to your elders or conclude counseling. We believe transformation happens through the ordinary means of grace (God's Word, prayer, sacraments, and community) pursued together, not in isolation.

Whole-life transformation

Not coping strategies, but a whole life ordered under Christ's lordship.

Read moreShow less

We don't just address symptoms or offer coping strategies. We help you develop a comprehensive Christian worldview, cultivate spiritual disciplines (including Scripture memory, meditation, and prayer), and order your entire life under Christ's lordship. The goal is Christlikeness and participation in His mission.

Conviction with openness

Clear convictions, gladly serving believers across denominational lines.

Read moreShow less

We hold clear theological convictions while serving believers across denominational lines. God's Word is for Christians of all stripes. Likewise, while we're grounded in Scripture's authority we also recognize God's good gifts through common grace, including the real benefits that medicine and science can provide. This means we can appreciate medical interventions while maintaining that Scripture interprets everything and that ultimate transformation comes through Christ alone.
